# Methodology — Channel Attribution & ROI

## Attribution Approach
Customer revenue is attributed to marketing channels using proportional purchase shares:
- WebShare
- CatalogShare
- StoreShare
- DealShare

Attributed revenue per channel is calculated as:

Channel Attributed Revenue = MntTotal × Channel Share

## Channel ROI
Channel-level ROI is calculated as:

ROI_channel = (Attributed Revenue − Channel Spend) / Channel Spend

## Purpose
This enables relative comparison of channel efficiency using available data.

## Caveat
This is a simplified, single-touch attribution proxy and does not capture multi-touch or assisted conversions.

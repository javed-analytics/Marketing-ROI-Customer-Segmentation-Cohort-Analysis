# Methodology — Marketing ROI & Sensitivity Analysis

## Objective
Assess overall marketing efficiency and understand how ROI behaves under different budget scenarios.

## ROI Definition
Overall ROI is calculated as:

ROI = (Total Revenue − Total Marketing Spend) / Total Marketing Spend

This captures how effectively marketing investment converts into revenue.

## Baseline Spend Assumption
The baseline marketing budget is assumed to be **7.7% of total revenue**, aligned with common retail benchmarks (7–10%).

## Sensitivity Analysis
Three scenarios are modeled:
- **Low Spend**: −20% from baseline
- **Base Spend**: baseline assumption
- **High Spend**: +20% from baseline

Revenue is held constant to isolate the impact of spend changes on ROI.

## Interpretation
A declining ROI at higher spend levels indicates diminishing returns. Results are directional, not predictive.

# Limitations & Assumptions

## No Transaction-Level Dates
Cohort value reflects cumulative spend to date rather than true monthly revenue curves.

## Simplified Attribution
Channel attribution uses proportional shares rather than multi-touch or causal attribution models.

## Static Revenue in Sensitivity Analysis
Revenue is held constant across spend scenarios, making the analysis directional rather than predictive.

## Snapshot-Based Recency
Recency is measured at a single point in time; averages may obscure individual variation.

## Segmentation Scope
Segmentation is based solely on spend and does not account for profitability, margin, or purchase frequency.
